# Understanding the National Debt

An interactive, nonpartisan primer on the U.S. federal budget, deficit and national debt, built for a neighborhood community-involvement group.

**Live site:** https://wrigh1ca-code.github.io/national-debt/

Everything is in one self-contained `index.html` (charts included), so it also works opened straight from a laptop with no internet connection.

Data: CBO, U.S. Treasury, BEA, IRS Statistics of Income (via Tax Foundation), Federal Reserve (FRED), IMF. Figures are for fiscal year 2026 unless noted; sources are listed at the bottom of the page.
